A memory I'll keep

That last day before distance

Some goodbyes feel different because you know things are about to change. That last day before going into a long-distance relationship became one of those memories.

When you're together, you don't always realize how precious ordinary time is. You think there will always be another tomorrow, another afternoon, another chance to sit together and talk.

Then suddenly distance becomes real, and you start wishing you had saved every little moment.

So I want to remember that day not only because it was a goodbye, but because it was proof of how many memories we had already made. And proof that one chapter ending didn't mean our story ended.

Different places don't have to mean different hearts.
